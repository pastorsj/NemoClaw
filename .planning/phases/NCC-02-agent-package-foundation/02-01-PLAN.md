<threat_model>

- **T-01-01 — Unauthorized support surface (high):** A technical plan is treated as product
  approval. Mitigation: blocking human checkpoint and exact accepted record.
- **T-01-02 — Scope substitution (high):** Untracked or later-edited proposal files differ from the
  revision named by the decision. Mitigation: accept only a committed proposal whose exact planning
  paths have no staged or working-tree difference, then record that revision in PROJECT.md.
- **T-01-03 — Capability inflation (medium):** Registration or a passing test is described as
  support. Mitigation: require the decision to separate availability, installation, selection,
  qualification, and support.

</threat_model>
