  Mitigation: store effective package authority separately.

  administrator recovery. Mitigation: preservation-only post-create rule and durable record tests.

  identity after Session replacement or pointer advancement. Mitigation: identity-bound record ID,
  exact durable reread, and no active/bundled fallback.
- **T-17-05 — Unsafe same-name recovery (critical):** Package work accidentally re-enables resume,
  reuse, recreation, or fresh onboarding for the retained name. Mitigation: unchanged entry gate and
  package-aware regression tests.
